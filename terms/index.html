Fax is not used as an order-receipt acknowledgment. Mark confirming copies “copy.”

Storage and handling

Most dry compounds can travel and be stored long-term at ambient temperature unless the quote says otherwise. Slightly cool, dark storage is preferred. After dissolution in DMSO, −20 °C or −80 °C is the usual long-term range. Solvent choice and storage are determined by the assay. TimTec is not responsible for quality issues that follow the customer’s in-house storage decisions.

Shipping and payment

Published freight for individual compounds: $70 domestic, $185 international. Terms: Net 30 days, FOB shipping point, subject to acceptance. No minimum order charge is maintained. Corporate Visa, Mastercard, and Discover are accepted (FAQ also lists American Express). Shipments go via UPS, FedEx, or another common carrier, insured at the buyer’s expense. Customer carrier accounts may be used collect. TimTec is not liable for carrier or customs delays, including spoilage in transit.

Partial shipment may be made at TimTec’s discretion unless the order forbids it. Visible damage should be reported to the carrier immediately; retain cartons until the claim is settled.

TimTec does not manufacture storage vessels; it sources plates and vials from outside suppliers and is not responsible for damage attributable to those vessels. Customers may ship their own vials or plates in advance of processing.

Shortage, returns, cancellation

Shortage claims: within 10 business days of receipt. Returns: only with authorization, within 30 days of the order date; items that deteriorated from incorrect storage or that are outdated are not accepted.

If an approved order is cancelled, the customer pays for incurred labor, materials, and services. Published cancellation fees: stock compounds 20% (minimum $50); overseas stock 30% (minimum $100); custom synthesis 50%. Fees may be waived when no cost has been incurred.
