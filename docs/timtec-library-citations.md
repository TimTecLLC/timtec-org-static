# TimTec Screening Library Citations (Verified)

Research date: 2026-09-06  
Scope: Peer-reviewed papers that **used or cite** the named TimTec collection. Prefer DOI links. Citations not invented — only included when library attribution is supported by the paper text, PMC/PubMed, and/or TimTec’s own publication pages **plus** independent DOI verification.

**Method notes**
- Seeds verified first (Shaneyfelt, Meiby, Duong-Thi, Barrow, Nielsen; Jadhav checked and **excluded** as NPL — see §1).
- Searches: Google/web, PubMed/PMC, TimTec product & “in publications” pages, DOI resolvers.
- “None found” means no credible peer-reviewed use of that **named** library was located after targeted search.

## 1. NPL-800 / TimTec Natural Product Library / NPL

### Verified citations

- **authors:** Shaneyfelt ME, Burke AD, Graff JW, Jutila MA, Hardy ME  
  **year:** 2006  
  **title:** Natural products that reduce rotavirus infectivity identified by a cell-based moderate-throughput screening assay  
  **journal:** Virology Journal  
  **doi_url:** https://doi.org/10.1186/1743-422X-3-68  
  **note:** Explicitly purchased TimTec natural product library (280 purified plant compounds at time of screen). Seed paper.

- **authors:** Nielsen AL, Kristensen LH, Stephansen KB, Kristensen JBL, Helgstrand C, Lees M, Cloos P, Helin K, Gajhede M, Olsen L  
  **year:** 2012  
  **title:** Identification of catechols as histone-lysine demethylase inhibitors  
  **journal:** FEBS Letters  
  **doi_url:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.febslet.2012.03.001  
  **note:** Screened a natural products library; TimTec identifies hit ST057529 (caffeic acid) as from NPL. Seed paper (vendor attribution of library ID).

- **authors:** Watson VG, Drake KM, Napper AD  
  **year:** 2013  
  **title:** Development of a high-throughput screening-compatible assay for the discovery of inhibitors of the AF4-AF9 interaction using AlphaScreen technology  
  **journal:** Assay and Drug Development Technologies  
  **doi_url:** https://doi.org/10.1089/adt.2012.495  
  **note:** Methods: “Natural Products Library (NPL) … from TimTec”; NPL = 680 natural products (paired with NDL in same pilot screen).

### Seed check — Jadhav et al. (NOT NPL)

- Jadhav A et al., *J Med Chem* 2010, https://doi.org/10.1021/jm901070c — screens ~197,861 NCGC/diverse compounds for cruzain aggregation artifacts. **Does not use TimTec NPL.** Listed on some TimTec marketing pages but **not a valid NPL citation**.
