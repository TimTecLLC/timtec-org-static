What we supply

TimTec designs and distributes synthetic organic and natural compounds for lead identification. Stock is held dry and can be freshly dissolved in DMSO.

R&D chemicals

ActiMol U.S. stock is 180,000 compounds, typically available within a few business days. Overseas inventory is larger and quoted with a longer lead time. Cherry-pick by structure, ID, or your own filters.

Screening collections

Diversity sets (ApexScreen, ActiProbe, ActiGlobe-50K, MS-10000), nature-informed and fragment libraries (NPL-800, NDL-3040, FL-500, FBL), and the ActiTarg targeted series. Sold complete, by plate, or as singles.
