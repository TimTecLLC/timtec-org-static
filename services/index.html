Services

Synthesis, plating, procurement, and cheminformatics

Milligram-to-kilogram custom work, library reformatting, and multi-supplier compound intake — quoted from the Kissimmee / Orlando office.

Custom organic synthesis

TimTec quotes custom synthesis from milligram to kilogram scale, including intermediates. Facilities have been described as available for active drug substances under cGMP requirements when that scope is agreed in the project plan. Inquiries are treated as confidential. Scale-up is scoped without promising a specific timeline until the route is reviewed.

Weighing and plating

Existing libraries can be reformatted for HTS. Technicians measure from about 0.10–10.00 mg into 96-well plates as powder or prepare DMSO solutions at a specified concentration. Dissolved samples are stored cold and can be shipped overnight on dry ice.

Compound management and acquisition

When a program buys from many suppliers, TimTec can act as a single receiving point: uniform repackaging, consistent labels and plate maps, and delivery in the vessel your assay group specified. Spot-checks of identity or quantity can be added to the SOW. Single projects and ongoing dry/liquid handling are both quoted.

Cheminformatics

Services listed by TimTec include chemical data management and data entry; IUPAC / SMILES / MOL conversion; diversity analysis and comparison of decks; heterocycle-focused diversity; and property calculations (drug-like filters, Lipinski, LogP, LogS, LogA). Programming and consulting are scoped per project. Related desktop software is offered separately at ChemDBsoft.com.

Email a structure file and the required amount to timtec@timtec.org. For a written quote, include scale, purity target, and any formatting constraints (vial, 96-well, 384-well, DMSO concentration).
