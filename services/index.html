Laboratory support

Services

TimTec provides custom synthesis, analytical work, and compound-management support for screening laboratories. Work is quoted from a written project plan. Inquiries are treated as confidential.

SDS Screening Collections Structure Search ID / SMILES

Service offerings

  1. 01 Custom synthesis

    Milligram to kilogram organic synthesis, intermediates, and active substances under cGMP when required.

  2. 02 Quality control

    Structure and purity by NMR and mass spectrometry. Stock compounds: typical average 95%, minimum 90%. 1H-NMR on request.

  3. 03 Analytical services

    HPLC method development and validation, finished-product, raw-material, and stability testing in an FDA- and DEA-registered laboratory.

  4. 04 NMR services

    Confirmation of customer or vendor samples. Capacity 5,000–10,000 spectra per month on six instruments (300–500 MHz). Printable and FID files.

  5. 05 Weighing and plating

    Dry or DMSO samples from a few vials to high throughput. 96- and 384-well plates, mother/daughter sets, 2-D barcoded tubes.

  6. 06 Liquid handling

    Tecan Freedom systems, 100 nL–5,000 µL. About 1.5 min to replicate a 96-well plate and under 5 min for a 384-well plate.

  7. 07 Multi-supplier acquisition

    Order, receive, QC, repackage, and deliver multi-source compounds in one format with customer database files.

  8. 08 Storage and shipping

    Short- and long-term chemical storage. Dry-ice domestic and international shipments of plates and vials.

  9. 09 Cheminformatics

    Diversity analysis, LogP, aqueous solubility (LogSw), absorption (FA), Lipinski filters, and database conversion (SDF / SMILES / MOL).

Weighing, plating, and library reformatting

TimTec formats existing collections for screening: dry weigh-out (typically 0.10–10 mg), DMSO solutions at a stated concentration, transfer from old vessels into robot-ready vials or plates, chemically resistant bar-coded labels, and electronic inventory (plate/well, amount, solvent, structure when not under CDA). Data are returned as spreadsheets or SD files. Small one-off jobs and ongoing monthly support are both accepted.

Analytical and NMR support

HPLC method development and validation, finished-product and raw-material testing, and stability studies are performed in an FDA- and DEA-registered laboratory. NMR confirmation is available for samples from your bank or from other vendors (5,000–10,000 spectra per month; 300–500 MHz). Stock compounds are checked on a rotating schedule for stability. Spectra can be supplied as printable files or FIDs with assignment.

Compound management and acquisition

When a screen needs compounds from several suppliers, TimTec can place the orders, receive and check shipments, spot-check identity and quantity by NMR, repackage into one vial or plate format, apply customer or sequential barcodes, and deliver a single database file (SD or customer-specified). Computational add-ons include diversity comparison, lipophilicity (LogP), solubility (LogSw), and absorption (FA) estimates.
