Diversity · 10,000 compounds

MyriaScreen II · MS-10000

A 10,000-compound diversity collection of high-purity, drug-like screening compounds from TimTec and Sigma-Aldrich stocks. Also referred to as MS-10000.

The MyriaScreen Diversity Collection of drug-like screening compounds was updated as MyriaScreen II, with over 50% new and more diverse structural material versus the original set. MyriaScreen II combines compounds from Sigma-Aldrich and TimTec stocks. The selection of 10,000 high-purity, diverse molecules is the result of multi-stage filtering and refinement of the two companies’ compound pools. TimTec order files have also used the MS-10000 label. Sigma-Aldrich historically listed the collection as Aldrich P/N T990000.

TimTec proprietary software was used to filter a pre-selected pool of over 300,000 TimTec and Sigma-Aldrich compounds on diversity and structural relevance for general screening. Additional filters considered physical-chemical properties including MW, cLogP, H-acceptors, H-donors, and rotatable bonds. The molecular-weight range is about 120 to 500. The collection is largely drug-like according to Lipinski’s Rule of 5 and also includes lead-like material, leaving room for molecular optimization.

The selection was refined to remove compounds that were overly represented or not well suited for medicinal chemistry follow-up.

Format and purity

MyriaScreen II is offered in DMSO plating formats. In addition to 1 mg/mL, a 10 mM concentration option is described on the legacy TimTec pages. Average purity is greater than 95%, with nothing below 90%. Re-supply from stock or re-synthesis is available for follow-up. Structural data and pricing are provided on request.

Example literature using MyriaScreen

Independent groups have reported use of the 10,000-compound MyriaScreen Diversity Collection. Examples (not product claims):

  • Barrow, E. W., Clinkenbeard, P. A., et al. High-throughput screening of a diversity collection using biodefense category A and B priority pathogens. J. Biomol. Screen. 2012, 17(7), 946–956. doi: 10.1177/1087057112448216. The 10,000-compound MyriaScreen Diversity Collection was screened against three NIAID category A pathogens and one category B pathogen.
  • Delgado-Soler, L., Toral, R., et al. RED: A set of molecular descriptors based on Rényi entropy. J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2009, 49(11), 2457–2468. doi: 10.1021/ci900275w.
