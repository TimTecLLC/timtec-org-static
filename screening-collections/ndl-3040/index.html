NDL-3040 Natural Derivatives Library

A crossover set of natural derivatives, analogs, semi-natural compounds, and mimics — 3,040 members, historically also called NDL-3000.

NDL-3040 (listed on older pages as NDL-3000) sits between purified natural products and fully synthetic space. Design draws on published natural-product scaffolds and in-house material: derivatives, analogs, semi-naturals, and mimics. The intent is to occupy chemical space that is still nature-informed but broader than strict drug-likeness cutoffs.

TimTec notes that natural-product scaffolds differ by organism of origin and that NDL includes scaffolds associated with plants, animals, and bacteria (see Ertl & Schuffenhauer, cheminformatics analysis of natural products, Progress in Drug Research, 2008, 66, 217–235).

Chemotype examples listed for the collection include alkaloids, natural phenols, nucleoside analogs, carbohydrates, purines and pyrimidines, flavonoids, steroids, and natural amino acids.

NDL is positioned as a structural complement to NPL-800. The two collections are independent; TimTec states there is no membership overlap. Available as the full set, subsets, or singles; vials or 96/384-well plates.

Literature context cited on the catalog (not TimTec trial data): reviews in J. Med. Chem. 2008 discussing natural products as drug leads (Ojima; Newman) remain the framing TimTec uses for this series.
