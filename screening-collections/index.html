Other series available on request

The live catalog also lists ActiTarg targeted sets (including CNS and ion-channel related collections), template-based heterocyclic groups, privileged-structure sets, a fragment-based library (FBL), ActiCom compounds with annotated activities, Diversity Set 10K, MyriaScreen (historically co-developed with Sigma-Aldrich), plant extracts, and gossypol derivatives. Ask for current plate maps and SDF files rather than assuming a legacy count is still exact.

Formatting

Typical delivery is dry solid or DMSO solution, in 1-dram (3.75 mL) vials or 96- / 384-well plates, at catalog or custom concentration. Contact TimTec for a free consultation on which collection fits the assay capacity and purpose.

Purity statement used across collections: generally at least 95% by high-field 1H-NMR and/or MS, minimum 90%. Re-supply from U.S. stock is offered for library members that remain in inventory.
