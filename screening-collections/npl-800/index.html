NPL-800 Natural Product Library

Pure natural compounds assembled as lead-identification material in a screen-ready format.

TimTec’s Natural Product Library (NPL) contains 800 purified natural compounds. The design goal is broad representation of selected natural material, not exclusive IP. Samples are primarily plant-sourced; the remainder come from bacteria, fungi, and animals. Common natural sources and literature pointers are available for most entries.

Natural molecules are often multi-active and frequently more bioavailable than a typical synthetic singleton. NPL is sold as a complete collection, by plate, or as individual compounds, dry or in DMSO at catalog or custom concentration.

Published screens that used NPL

These are literature reports, not TimTec efficacy claims:

  • Shaneyfelt, M. E., et al. Natural products that reduce rotavirus infectivity identified by a cell-based moderate-throughput screening assay. Virology Journal, 2006, 3:68. The NPL subset then numbered 280 compounds; 15 showed antiviral activity without cytotoxicity in that assay.
  • Bisson, W. H., et al. Modeling of the Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor (AhR) ligand-binding domain and its use in virtual ligand screening. J. Med. Chem. 2009, 52(18), 5635–5641. (Customer note on file that NPL was used in a physical and virtual screen.)
  • Jadhav, A., et al. Quantitative analyses of aggregation, autofluorescence, and reactivity artifacts in a thiol-protease screen. J. Med. Chem. 2010, 53(1), 37–51.
  • Nielsen, A. L., et al. Identification of catechols as histone-lysine demethylase inhibitors. FEBS Lett. 2012, 586(8), 1190–1194. ST057529 from NPL was reported as an inhibitor of ccKDM4C.

Related collections

NDL-3040 extends the same design idea into derivatives and mimics; TimTec states there is no overlap between NPL and NDL membership. See also FL-500.

Purchasing note: NPL is built mainly from known natural compounds that are also sold by other suppliers. TimTec does not claim IP on library members. Products are for R&D use and should be handled by trained personnel.
