NPL-800 Natural Product Library

Pure natural compounds assembled as lead-identification material in a screen-ready format.

TimTec’s Natural Product Library (NPL) contains 800 purified natural compounds. The design goal is broad representation of selected natural material, not exclusive IP. Samples are primarily plant-sourced; the remainder come from bacteria, fungi, and animals. Common natural sources and literature pointers are available for most entries.

Natural molecules are often multi-active and frequently more bioavailable than a typical synthetic singleton. NPL is sold as a complete collection, by plate, or as individual compounds, dry or in DMSO at catalog or custom concentration.

Published screens that used NPL

These are literature reports, not TimTec efficacy claims:

  • Shaneyfelt, M. E.; Burke, A. D.; Graff, J. W.; Jutila, M. A.; Hardy, M. E. Natural products that reduce rotavirus infectivity identified by a cell-based moderate-throughput screening assay. Virol. J. 2006, 3:68. doi:10.1186/1743-422X-3-68. TimTec natural product library; 280 purified plant compounds at the time of the screen.
  • Bisson, W. H.; Koch, D. C.; O’Donnell, E. F.; Khalil, S. M.; Kerkvliet, N. I.; Tanguay, R. L.; Abagyan, R.; Kolluri, S. K. Modeling of the aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR) ligand binding domain and its utility in virtual ligand screening to predict new AhR ligands. J. Med. Chem. 2009, 52(18), 5635–5641. doi:10.1021/jm900199u. Virtual screen of 498 TimTec natural compounds (NPL-class / TimTec naturals; the paper does not name NPL-800).
  • Nielsen, A. L.; et al. Identification of catechols as histone-lysine demethylase inhibitors. FEBS Lett. 2012, 586(8), 1190–1194. doi:10.1016/j.febslet.2012.03.001. ST057529 (caffeic acid) from NPL.
  • Watson, V. G.; Drake, K. M.; Napper, A. D. Development of a high-throughput screening-compatible assay for the discovery of inhibitors of the AF4-AF9 interaction using AlphaScreen technology. Assay Drug Dev. Technol. 2013. doi:10.1089/adt.2012.495. Methods name TimTec NPL (680 natural products at that screen), paired with NDL.
  • Chan, J. D.; Day, T. A.; Marchant, J. S. Coalescing beneficial host and deleterious antiparasitic actions as an antischistosomal strategy. eLife 2018, 7:e35755. doi:10.7554/eLife.35755. Screened TimTec NPL-800 and NDL-3000 alongside an NCI natural-product set.
  • Yang, K.; Yin, J.; Yue, X.; Bieber, K.; Riemekasten, G.; Ludwig, R. J.; Petersen, F.; Yu, X. Luteolin peracetate and gossypolone inhibit immune complex-mediated neutrophil activation in vitro and dermal-epidermal separation in an ex vivo model of epidermolysis bullosa acquisita. Front. Immunol. 2023, 14, 1196116. doi:10.3389/fimmu.2023.1196116. Natural product library obtained from TimTec (NPL-800).
