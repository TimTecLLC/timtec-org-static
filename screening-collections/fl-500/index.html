FL-500 Flavonoid Derivatives

A focused 500-compound set built from nine core flavonoid lattices, offered with custom formatting and cherry-picking.

Flavonoids are a preferred natural chemotype in many phenotypic and target screens: they are abundant in edible plants and are frequently studied for anti-inflammatory, anticancer, and antioxidant readouts. TimTec assembled FL-500 around nine core structures:

  • Chalcone
  • Flavanone
  • Flavone
  • Dihydroflavonol
  • Flavonol
  • Flavan
  • Stilbene
  • Isoflavonoid
  • Neoflavonoid

Custom formatting and cherry-picking are available. SDF access is by request.

Screens and reviews involving flavonoids

  • Narwal, M., et al. Screening and structural analysis of flavones inhibiting tankyrases. J. Med. Chem. 2013, 56(9), 3507–3517.
  • Holder, S., et al. Characterization of a potent and selective small-molecule inhibitor of PIM1 kinase. Mol. Cancer Ther. 2007, 6(1), 163–172; see also Bullock et al., J. Med. Chem. 2005, 48(24), 7604–7614.
  • Auld, D. S., et al. Characterization of chemical libraries for luciferase inhibitory activity. J. Med. Chem. 2008, 51(8), 2372–2386.

These citations illustrate the chemotype’s use in the literature; they are not a guarantee that FL-500 members match any particular published hit.
