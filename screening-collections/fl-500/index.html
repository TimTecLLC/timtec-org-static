Nature-informed · 500 compounds

FL-500 Flavonoid Derivatives

A focused 500-compound set built from nine core flavonoid lattices, offered with custom formatting and cherry-picking.

Flavonoids are a preferred natural chemotype in many phenotypic and target screens: they are abundant in edible plants and are frequently studied for anti-inflammatory, anticancer, and antioxidant readouts. TimTec assembled FL-500 around nine core structures:

  • Chalcone
  • Flavanone
  • Flavone
  • Dihydroflavonol
  • Flavonol
  • Flavan
  • Stilbene
  • Isoflavonoid
  • Neoflavonoid

Custom formatting and cherry-picking are available. SDF access is by request.

Published screens

These papers used TimTec’s 500-compound flavonoid set (nine cores). Literature reports, not TimTec efficacy claims:

  • Manner, S.; Skogman, M.; Goeres, D. M.; Vuorela, P.; Fallarero, A. Systematic exploration of natural and synthetic flavonoids for the inhibition of Staphylococcus aureus biofilms. Int. J. Mol. Sci. 2013, 14(10), 19434–19451. doi:10.3390/ijms141019434. Explicit TimTec collection of 500 synthetic and natural flavonoids (nine cores).
  • Narwal, M.; Haikarainen, T.; Fallarero, A.; Vuorela, P. M.; Lehtiö, L. Screening and structural analysis of flavones inhibiting tankyrases. J. Med. Chem. 2013, 56(9), 3507–3517. doi:10.1021/jm3018783. Screened 500 natural and naturally derived flavonoids covering nine flavonoid classes.
