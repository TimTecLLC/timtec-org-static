Diversity · 5,040 compounds

ApexScreen

A starter-scale diversity collection drawn from TimTec stock, including a subset of naturals and natural-product derivatives.

ApexScreen is 5,040 compounds selected to represent the diversity of TimTec stock (historically described as 160,000+ compounds) in a smaller format than 10K+ decks. TimTec assembled it with in-house diversity-analysis software from U.S. and overseas inventory, with optional filters for MW, cLogP, and Lipinski parameters.

Besides diverse synthetic chemotypes, ApexScreen includes a number of pure natural compounds from the Natural Product Library. Formatting (mass or concentration) is customizable. Structural data and pricing are provided on request.

Published screens that used ApexScreen

These are literature reports, not TimTec efficacy claims:

  • LeVine, H., III. Identification of an inhibitor of tau aggregation. Alzheimers Dement. 2008, 4(4 Suppl.), T474. doi:10.1016/j.jalz.2008.05.1418. Meeting abstract; names Apexscreen 5040.
  • Kenwood, B. M.; et al. Identification of a novel mitochondrial uncoupler that does not depolarize the plasma membrane. Mol. Metab. 2014, 3(2), 114–123. doi:10.1016/j.molmet.2013.11.005. ApexScreen 5040 purchased from TimTec; BAM15 / ST056388.
  • Sheen, C.-W.; Alptürk, O.; Sluis-Cremer, N. Novel high-throughput screen identifies an HIV-1 reverse transcriptase inhibitor with a unique mechanism of action. Biochem. J. 2014, 462, 425–432. doi:10.1042/BJ20140365. ApexScreen purchased from TimTec; hit APEX57219.
  • LeVine, H., III; Lampe, L.; Abdelmoti, L.; Augelli-Szafran, C. E. Dihydroxybenzoic acid isomers differentially dissociate soluble biotinyl-Aβ(1–42) oligomers. Biochemistry 2012, 51(38), 7428–7435. doi:10.1021/bi201288x. Follow-on chemistry from the ApexScreen Aβ-oligomer screen.
