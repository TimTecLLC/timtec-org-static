About

A privately held U.S. company, veteran- and woman-owned

TimTec LLC was founded in 1995 near the University of Delaware in Newark to acquire and distribute synthetic organic and natural compounds and to support custom synthesis, software, and laboratory work for drug discovery.

The company is now based in the Orlando area at 1950 East Irlo Bronson Memorial Highway, Suite 301, Kissimmee, FL 34744. Operations include compound distribution, custom weighing and microplate preparation, and quality control that includes NMR analysis. Older pages still mention a prior Tampa address; use the Kissimmee address for shipping and correspondence.

TimTec designs screening libraries of different sizes and specializations and also provides compound management, custom formatting, and computational services.

Purpose

The stated mission is to serve current chemistry needs of the life-science R&D community — with the working assumption that a molecule may become a therapeutic, a cosmetic ingredient, or another applied material. Flexibility in sourcing and formatting is treated as part of the business model.

Effective January 2023, TimTec states that it voluntarily provides a 10–15% discount on orders directed to nonprofit organizations, U.S. government agencies, and universities worldwide. Confirm the rate on the quote.

Selected history (from TimTec’s published timeline)

  • 1993–1995: U.S. incorporation and establishment of TimTec in Delaware; ActiMol library begun.
  • 1997–1998: Compound-transfer services; ChemDBsoft offered; custom synthesis and analytical work expanded.
  • 1999–2001: Natural products emphasis; liquid-handling robotics; plant extracts added.
  • 2004–2005: Collaborations including MaxiVerse with Biomol and MyriaScreen with Sigma-Aldrich.
  • 2009–2011: Flavonoid and fragment libraries; eChemStore; MyriaScreen II.

Customer geography reported for 2022 sales: United States and Canada about 50%, Europe 35%, Asia Pacific and South America 15%, across a stated base of roughly 1,800 individual customers and 8,000 accounts. Partnerships named on the Network page include Sigma-Aldrich / MilliporeSigma / Merck (MyriaScreen; LOPAC®1280 formatting projects).
