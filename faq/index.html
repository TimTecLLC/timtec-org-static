FAQ

Ordering, data files, and shipping

I need an SDF file

Library SDFs are provided through TimTec’s database-download process or by email. If a public download link is required, ask timtec@timtec.org for the current route. TT-BOT can help navigate catalog questions.

How do I search compounds?

Use Structure Search or ID / SMILES, or email a list with required amounts in Excel, SDF, SMILES, or MOL.

I cannot find an ID, order, CAS, or catalog number

Not all compounds are listed online. TimTec can quote custom synthesis for a structure or substructure you send.

How do I place an order?

Email orders@timtec.org or support@timtec.net, fax 302-292-8520, or call 302-292-8500. Say whether partial shipments are acceptable and include an email for carrier notifications. Approved business accounts can also order and download CoA/SDS files in the Customer Portal.

Payment

ACH, wire transfer, company check, and Visa / Mastercard / American Express. Net 30 is offered on approved accounts (see Terms). W-9 and vendor-form requests: call 302-292-8500.

Shipping cost and origin

TimTec can waive listed freight if you provide a FedEx or DHL account. Published basic freight is $70 domestic U.S. and $185 international for individual-compound shipments. Carriers used include FedEx and UPS. Shipments go out from the Kissimmee / Orlando-area operation. Refrigerated items are generally not booked for Friday dispatch unless you ask.

Plating and storage

Compounds are stored dry and can be freshly dissolved. Delivery vessels include 1-dram (3.75 mL) vials and 96- or 384-well plates. Store in a dark, cool place; some lots need refrigerator or freezer conditions — TimTec will flag those. DMSO solutions are typically held at −20 °C or −80 °C; freeze–thaw cycles can affect stability.

Very small or very large amounts

TimTec states it will try to meet requests from below 1 mg up to multi-gram, and will quote kilogram-scale custom synthesis separately.

File-format conversion

SDF import into ISIS/Base or other desktop tools is a customer-side step (create/open a database, map fields, import). Excel cannot be converted directly to JC format; import to a chemistry database first. TimTec can quote data-conversion work. ChemDBsoft is TimTec’s own database package for SDF/MOL/SMILES workflows.
