R&D Chemicals

Individual compounds and custom selections

In addition to pre-designed libraries, you can choose any number of samples from TimTec screening stock using your own criteria. Compounds from the named collections are also available for cherry-picking.

ActiMol U.S. stock

180,000 synthetic organic compounds held for short lead times (the current site quotes 1–3 business days for this pool). Available as singles in vials or as custom plate maps.

Extended / overseas stock

1.5 million additional compounds. Delivery is typically quoted in weeks rather than days, depending on the source lot. Confirm timing on the quote.

Drug-like filters

Compounds placed into pre-designed libraries are filtered on physicochemical properties used in hit-to-lead work: cLogP, molecular weight, and hydrogen-bond donor/acceptor counts as framed by Lipinski’s Rule of Five. Diversity selections can be further constrained to your MW, cLogP, and related cutoffs, or assembled so they add chemotypes that are missing from an existing customer deck.

Quality control

TimTec reports structure and purity by NMR and mass spectrometry. Average purity is 95%; the stated minimum is 90%. Stock is spot-checked for stability. 1H-NMR spectra for stock compounds are available on request.

Compounds are stored dry and freshly prepared in DMSO when a solution format is ordered. TimTec prefers hand-synthesized, non-combinatorial designs when assembling libraries, mixing in-house synthesis with outside sourcing to keep collection-to-collection diversity high.

Catalog search

Use the live tools (new tab) to look up structures and identifiers. Not every lot is listed online; email a list in Excel, SDF, SMILES, or MOL if you cannot find an ID or CAS.
